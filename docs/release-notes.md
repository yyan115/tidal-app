OpenTune 0.2.0 brings the Gallery design to the whole app.

- Rename the application to OpenTune, including its launcher, notification mark, settings and documentation.
- Use an original line-icon family, editorial headings, deep black surfaces and a floating player throughout the interface.
- Choose from seven colour palettes with separate light, dark and system appearance. Theme choices persist across launches.
- Inspect the actual codec, bit depth and sample rate from the player. These describe the source, rather than assuming the connected audio device's output format.
- Keep playback, queue editing, downloads, exports and browser sign-in available in the redesigned interface.

Install this APK over version 0.1.1 to retain your library, downloads and account session. Android 8 or later is required; Google Play Services are not required.

TIDAL integration is unofficial. Protected streams are unsupported. Physical GrapheneOS devices, USB DACs, Bluetooth devices and long-running battery behavior remain unverified.
