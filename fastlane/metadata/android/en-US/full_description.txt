OpenTune is an unofficial, open source Android music player for TIDAL. It also plays your own audio files.

OpenTune has no ads, analytics or tracking, and it connects only to TIDAL.

<ul>
<li>Stream TIDAL in lossless FLAC, up to 24-bit/192 kHz where TIDAL provides it.</li>
<li>Search the catalogue and browse your saved tracks, albums and playlists. Create and edit TIDAL playlists.</li>
<li>See the codec, bit depth and sample rate of the playing track.</li>
<li>Download tracks and play them without a connection or an account.</li>
<li>Play local audio files and combine them with TIDAL tracks in playlists.</li>
<li>Edit the queue, shuffle and repeat. Control playback from the notification, the lock screen or a Bluetooth device.</li>
<li>Use offline mode and Wi-Fi-only downloads.</li>
</ul>

You sign in on TIDAL's website in your browser. OpenTune never receives your password.

OpenTune is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by TIDAL. Streaming requires a TIDAL subscription. TIDAL can change or block the interfaces OpenTune uses without notice.
